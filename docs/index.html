How to log a trade from Trading 212
  1. In Trading 212, open the order. Tap the stock, scroll down to History and tap the buy or sell, or use the History tab.
  2. Copy Quantity into Shares. Fractions like 0.187 are fine.
  3. Copy the £ total of the order into Total. For a sell, that's the £ you got back.
  4. Type the ticker and tap the right company when it appears. London shares end in .L, so Tesco is TSCO.L, not TSCO (that's Tractor Supply in the US).
  5. Ignore the "Average price" and any $ or p prices. The site works out the £ price per share for you. If your order shows a slightly different £ price per share, you can type over it.

Made a mistake? Find it in the Trade log at the bottom, tap Delete twice, and enter it again.
